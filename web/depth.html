Tacitum / Pool depth

How much money is really in this pool?

An agent needs that answer before it trades, lends, lists a token or moves a treasury through a Solana pool. On Telegraph it was the most-failed question on the network: 603 asks in three days, 32% usable, and ten pools asked about 402 times with no answer at all. This miner reads the reserves straight from chain, shows its work, and says so when nobody can know.

How it answers

Shows its work, declines instead of guessing.

Read from chain

Only token vaults the pool account owns, or the two vaults a Raydium or PumpSwap pool names in its own data. Tokens spammed to the pool address are ignored.

Every number re-checkable

Each answer lists the vault accounts, amounts, prices and Solana slot, so anyone can read the same accounts and get the same figure.

Route and depth

Jupiter quotes restricted to that DEX confirm trades go through this exact pool, and give the price impact of a $1k, $10k and $100k trade.

Honest declines

Scorch, Manifest, Deriverse, JupLend and Sanctum keep funds outside the pool. Generic methods were off by up to 100 times there, so the miner says why and returns no number.
