Tacitum / Screened pool

Private funding, clean by construction.

A private pool on a network whose busiest job is sanctions screening has to keep sanctioned money out. Every depositor is screened through Telegraph before their deposit can be spent, and the screening receipt is written on chain with the approval.

Screening log

Every approval carries its receipt.

Click any receipt to open Telegraph's public record of the screen. The last row is a screen test against a wallet on the OFAC list: it is flagged, so a deposit from it would be refused and refunded.
