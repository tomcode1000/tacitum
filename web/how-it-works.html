Tacitum / How it works

Two leaks, two fixes.

Telegraph publishes every paid question and the wallet that paid for it. Tacitum hides the question among decoys and hides the payer behind a pool that only screened money can enter. Nothing is taken away from verification.

Leak one: the question

Who you are screening

Fix: matched decoys. The real company is bought together with three others chosen to look the same on every public signal: how famous they are, how the name is written, what the registry says and how much they file. The four checks are shuffled and spaced, and decoy groups are fixed, so repeat screens give nothing away.

Leak two: the payer

Which wallet is asking

Fix: the screened pool. Each assessment is paid by a brand-new wallet. Its Telegraph balance comes from a pool contract through a zero-knowledge withdraw, so on chain the payer is funded by the pool, never by the desk.
