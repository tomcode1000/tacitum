    Screened pool

    The payer is funded by a pool only screened money can enter.

    A private pool on a network whose busiest job is sanctions screening has to be clean by construction. Every depositor is screened through Telegraph before their deposit can be spent, and the screening receipt is stored on chain with the approval.
